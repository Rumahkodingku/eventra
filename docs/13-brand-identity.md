# 13 — Brand Identity

**Status:** FINAL — approved project identity baseline v1.1

## Brand

- Name: **Eventra**
- Tagline: **Your Gateway to Every Experience.**
- Product descriptor: Premium multi-category event discovery and ticketing platform.
- Project owner/showcase: Rumah Kodingku.

## Brand idea

Eventra is a gateway to live experiences: music, sport, culture, learning and entertainment. The identity should feel premium and energetic while remaining clear and dependable during transactional journeys.

## Personality

Confident, curated, contemporary, expressive, trustworthy and inclusive.

## Visual identity

- Art direction: Premium Event Editorial.
- Primary brand color: Electric Violet `#7357F6`.
- Premium dark foundation: Deep Midnight `#171B2B`.
- Main light canvas: Warm Off-white `#F7F6F2`.
- Main text: Ink Black `#111318`.
- Accent highlight: Lime `#C7F36B`.
- Supporting colors: Pure White `#FFFFFF`, Muted Slate `#737987`, Soft Violet `#EEEAFE`, Border `#E5E5E2`.

## Color usage rules

- Violet is the primary interactive brand accent for CTA, active navigation and selected controls.
- Deep Midnight is used for premium hero regions, navigation and dark surfaces.
- Warm Off-white is the default light canvas; white is used for cards and elevated form surfaces.
- Lime is a rare highlight for featured labels and promotional emphasis. It must not become a competing primary CTA color.
- Ink Black is used for text on light surfaces and on lime surfaces.
- Status colors (success, warning, danger, info) communicate system meaning and are not brand accents.
- Verify WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for each actual foreground/background pairing.

## Typography

- Recommended UI family: Inter or equivalent.
- Use expressive bold weights for display headings.
- Use readable regular/medium weights for body and utility UI.
- Use tabular numerals for prices, order references and analytics.

## Logo assets

Approved files:

- Light wordmark: `public/logo/eventra-light-mode-bg-transparent.png`
- Dark wordmark: `public/logo/eventra-dark-mode-bg-transparent.png`
- Mark: `public/logo/eventra-logo-mark.png`

Usage:

- Use the light wordmark on warm off-white and white surfaces.
- Use the dark wordmark on Deep Midnight surfaces such as the footer, organizer sidebar, and dark panels.
- Use the mark for favicon, app icon, compact organizer mobile header, and small spaces.
- Keep the wordmark's aspect ratio. Do not stretch, recolor, outline, or place it on a conflicting background.
- Maintain clear space around the wordmark. Minimum header height is 36px; footer and auth can use 44–48px.
- Provide alt text "Eventra — Your Gateway to Every Experience." for the full wordmark. Compact mark links use the accessible name "Eventra home".
- Organizer profiles use monogram initials, not the Eventra platform logo.

## Logo direction

The approved mark is a geometric portal/ticket form with violet depth and a lime highlight dot. Keep the mark recognizable at favicon size and avoid adding effects beyond the supplied artwork.

## Voice and copy

- Clear, concise, warm and confident.
- Prefer direct action labels.
- Avoid false urgency and unsupported claims.
- Label simulated payment, demo inventory and sample analytics transparently.

## Brand governance

- Centralize name, tagline, logo paths and palette tokens.
- Do not introduce new accent colors without updating this document and Design System.
- Eventra is approved as the project brand name. Domain and trademark availability have not been checked.
